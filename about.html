01 — Our story

One room,one curve,eleven years.

Ooha Arts started in 2014 as a two-person studio above a bakery in Indiranagar, with one camera, one light and a colour profile that had been stolen from a film lab.

That profile is still the backbone of everything we make. It has been rebuilt maybe a dozen times, but it has never been abandoned — and it is why a wedding album from 2016 sits on the same wall as a product catalogue from last month without looking like a different studio made them.

Today we are a small, deliberately fixed team: two photographers, two editors, a producer who keeps the schedules honest, and a print room that still smells faintly of fixer. We take on a limited number of commissions so that every file gets the attention it needs.

02 — How we work

Four values werefuse to trade away.

01

Honest about limits

If a location will not work at 4pm in May, we say so before you pay. We have turned down more bookings than we have taken.

02

Owned, in-house editing

Nothing is outsourced to a factory. The person who shot it is the person who grades it, end to end.

03

Files that last

16-bit masters, layered PSDs, and an archive of your project we keep for ten years. The hard drive will fail. We will not.

04

No unnecessary faces

A fixed team of collaborators we trust, instead of a roster of strangers. Your photographer is never swapped out.

03 — Milestones

Eleven years,compressed.

2014 — The first room

Two cameras above a bakery in Indiranagar. Eleven weddings a year and a borrowed colour profile.

2017 — The print room

We stopped sending files to a lab we could not visit. Fine-art printing, album binding and framing came in-house.

2020 — The edit team

A dedicated colour and retouching department, plus the first version of the pipeline we still use for bulk product work today.

2023 — Field Notes

Our first self-published book, printed in three runs. It remains the project we are proudest of and the least asked about.

Today — Still small

Six people, one studio, a limited number of commissions, and a waiting list we mean to keep small.

04 — The team

Six people.No agency layer.

Everyone who touches your files works in the same room. That is the entire point.
