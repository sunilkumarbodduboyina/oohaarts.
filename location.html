03 — Getting here

Travel notes,honestly.

By air

Kempegowda Airport

35 km, roughly 60 minutes outside peak hours. We recommend a pre-booked car over the airport taxi queue. From MG Road it is 15 minutes.

By metro

puttur

Located in Puttur, Andhra Pradesh, with convenient access to nearby roads, shops, and local transportation. Easily accessible by auto-rickshaw or on foot, depending on your starting point. Parking availability may vary, especially on Sundays and during busy hours.

On location

We come to you

Most commissions are shot on location across Bengaluru — palaces, factories, coffee estates, beaches. Travel within the city is included in every package.

Nearby

Worth the walkbefore you leave.

The jewellers we shot for Halo. Goldsmiths, still working the old benches, three minutes on foot.

Where the Ash & Ember vessels were made. Ask for Anu; tell her we sent you.

The client behind Chromatic Silence. Tastings on Saturdays, and they let us in early to use the window light.

The tree cover that gives the area its name. Our most-used location for morning portraits.
