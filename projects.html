A note on selection

Why onlyforty images?

Because nine thousand photographs is not a gift, it is a chore. A wedding day of forty keepers, edited as one continuous sequence, is worth more to you than four hundred that all say the same thing.

It also takes three times as long, which is why the schedule in the brief always says seven days and never three.
