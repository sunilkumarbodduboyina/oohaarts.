Process

From first messageto final print.

The same five steps apply whether it is a single sitting or a three-week campaign.

Enquiry & availability

Send the date, the place and what you need. Within a few hours you get a straight yes or no, and a range if it is a yes.

Treatment & quote

A written plan: light, timings, crew, wardrobe, deliverables, usage rights. You approve it before any deposit.

The shoot

One direction, unhurried, with a printed call sheet. Same-day edits go out that evening for events.

Edit & retouch

Full graded edit within seven days. You get a proof gallery to review, with one round of revisions included.

Prints & handover

Masters, web set and social crops by day seven. Albums and prints inside twenty-one days, collected or delivered.
